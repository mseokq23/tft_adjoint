Alternative parameter sets for the 2%-W BG-IWO model of arXiv:2609.12184 (Step 5)
Cross-check package for running the sets in another simulator (e.g. a Sentaurus deck)
================================================================================

alternative_parameter_sets.csv is generated by scripts/make_crosscheck_package.py
from stored results (results/e5_nd, results/e9a, results/e9b, results/e8); no
simulation is needed to rebuild it. step5_stack_vth.json (Step-5 Vth of the
thicker stacks, quoted in check 4 below) comes from the same script with
--stack_vth, which runs three short DEVSIM sweeps.

What the sets are
-----------------
We implemented the same descriptor class in an open-source 2D drift-diffusion
model (DEVSIM) and used the published Step-5 parameters as the "truth": the
2%-W calibration curves (L = 1 um, VD = 0.05 and 1.0 V) were simulated with the
Step-5 values, and these simulated curves were then re-fitted with ND,eff held
at a different value and the other descriptors free. Qf was held at the Step-5
value because it is exactly degenerate with the gate WF.

  Sets A-E   ND,eff from 7.9e15 to 7.9e18 cm^-3 (1000x). They reproduce the
             simulated Step-5 calibration curves within RMS <= 1.3e-3 decade, and
             their Vth at the five transfer conditions (L = 1 um at
             VD = 0.1/0.2/0.5 V; L = 2 um at VD = 0.05/1.0 V) stays within
             0.8 mV of Step 5.
  Set G      ND,eff = 2.5e19 cm^-3, a control. It is rejected by the calibration
             curves (chi2 = 188 at 0.02 decade noise), yet its Vth at the five
             transfer conditions stays within 14 mV of Step 5. W_TA sits at the
             0.30 eV fit bound and sigma_GD = 0.0201 eV just above the 0.02 eV bound.
             Its DOS values depend on the fit path (a shorter fit gave the same chi2
             with log10 N_GD 17.00 and E_GD 0.50 eV), so treat set G as a control only.
  Set F      Exact identity: the donor-like Gaussian replaced by the same Gaussian
             as acceptor-like, and its integrated density added to ND,eff.
             N(1-f) = N - N*f, so I-V and C-V are identical (in our model the
             differences are < 1e-14 decade in ID and < 2e-14 relative in C-V).

alternative_parameter_sets.csv lists all sets. The columns starting with
"devsim_" are results from our model, all relative to the Step-5 reference:
  chi2 over the 64 calibration points (VD = 0.05/1 V, VG step 0.1 V, ID >= 10 pA,
  sigma = 0.02 decade), RMS and max deviation of log10(ID) on those points
  worst RMS deviation and max |dVth| over the five transfer curves
  predicted dVth for a device with t_IWO = 7 nm and with t_ox = 14 nm (VD = 1 V)
  predicted max change of the quasi-static gate C-V (% of Cox*A)
Vth is the gate voltage at ID = 100 nA. "n/a": not evaluated (set G is already
rejected by the calibration curves).

Definitions as implemented (map these to the target deck)
---------------------------------------------------------
  Gaussian donor-like DOS   g_D = N_GD * exp(-(dE - E_GD)^2 / (2 sigma_GD^2)),
                            dE = Ec - E >= 0 (truncated at Ec)
                            N_GD = NDOS, E_GD = Ec - (Gaussian centre), sigma_GD = sigma
  Acceptor-like tail        g_A = N_TA * exp(-dE / W_TA)
                            N_TA = Ntail (Etail = 0), W_TA = sigma_tail
  Step-5 reference values   as stated in Sec. III-A: NDOS 2.19e19 cm^-3 eV^-1 at
                            Ec - 0.12 eV, sigma 0.08 eV; Ntail 7.8e19 cm^-3 eV^-1,
                            sigma_tail 0.133 eV; WF S/D 4.16 eV, gate WF 4.00 eV,
                            Qf +1.59e10 cm^-2, ND,eff 2.56e18 cm^-3, mu0 24 cm^2/Vs.
                            (Sec. IV quotes the 2%-W Gaussian centre as Ec - 0.122 eV;
                            we used 0.12 eV.)
  Trap occupancy            quasi-equilibrium with the conduction band:
                            f = n / (n + n1), n1 = Nc exp(-dE/kT); 40 energy levels over 0-1.6 eV
  ND,eff                    uniform, fully ionized donor density in the IWO layer
  Qf                        sheet charge at the gate-dielectric/IWO interface
  Material (assumed, not given in the paper)
                            chi = 4.16 eV, Eg = 3.05 eV, Nc = 5e18 cm^-3,
                            eps(IWO) = 10, eps(HfO2) = 20
  Transport                 electrons only, Boltzmann statistics, constant mobility mu0
  Contacts                  gate: work-function contact under the HfO2 over the full length;
                            S/D: on top of the IWO over the 1.5 um overlaps,
                            n = Nc exp(-(WF - chi)/kT)
  Structure                 Table III of the paper (IWO 3.5 nm, HfO2 7 nm,
                            L = 1 / 2 um, W = 5 um, S/D overlap 1.5 um); the 5 nm
                            HfO2 passivation is modelled over the channel gap only

Absolute values do not transfer between simulators. With the published Step-5
values, Vth in our model is 0.16-0.21 V higher than the measured calibration
curves (+156 mV at VD = 0.05 V, +208 mV at VD = 1 V; results/e10), while the
paper's Sentaurus model stays within 36.1 mV over the drain-bias split.
The meaningful comparison is relative: whether sets A-F reproduce the Step-5
curves of the target deck.

Why thickness and C-V address the ambiguity
-------------------------------------------
Origin. In a 3.5 nm channel, a uniform ND,eff acts approximately like a sheet
charge ND,eff*t_IWO at the gate interface and shifts Vth by about
q*ND,eff*t_IWO/C_ox, much as Qf and the gate WF do. From set A to set E the gate
WF moves by +170 mV, close to the +176 mV change in q*ND,eff*t_IWO/C_ox; the DOS
terms absorb the rest. The I-V data mainly constrain the net charge versus
surface potential within the Fermi-level window covered by the sweep (about
Ec - 0.35 eV to Ec for ID >= 10 pA in our model).

Transfer tests. Drain bias and channel length change the lateral transport
rather than the vertical electrostatics, so they do little to separate these
contributions. Given the calibration curves, the predicted 1-sigma of each
transfer test is only 0.25-0.43 of its noise (RMS over the curve, 0.02 decade;
linearized at Step 5, Qf fixed).

Vertical-stack tests. They change what the calibration cannot see:
  t_ox     C_ox changes, so sheet-like charge (Qf, and ND,eff through its
           sheet-like effect) shifts Vth by a different amount than the WF does
  t_IWO    the volume charge (ND,eff and the DOS scale with thickness) changes,
           while Qf and the WF do not
  C-V      quasi-static gate C-V measures the charge directly, independent of
           mobility and contacts
Their predicted 1-sigma relative to their noise is 118 (t_ox = 14 nm), 239
(t_IWO = 7 nm) and 32 (C-V with 0.5 % of C_ox*A noise). Adding all three reduces
the linearized 1-sigma of ND,eff from 2.95 to 0.44 decade and of N_TA from 2.37
to 0.14 decade. The donor/acceptor identity (set F) is not broken by any of them:
it stays exact for any electrical measurement within this descriptor class.

Nonlinear check (columns devsim_dVth_* and devsim_max_CV_dev_pct_Cox): the
sets A-E differ from Step 5 by 18-71 mV in Vth for t_ox = 14 nm and 33-190 mV
for t_IWO = 7 nm (VD = 1 V), and by 1.3-6.9 % of C_ox*A in quasi-static C-V
(VD = 0), against <= 0.8 mV at the five transfer conditions.

Caveats. These are model predictions. Real C-V of oxide TFTs is frequency
dependent (the quasi-static limit assumes the traps follow the signal), and a
thickness split assumes the IWO and its interfaces are otherwise unchanged.

Suggested checks in another simulator
-------------------------------------
1. Run sets A-F in the Step-5 deck (only the listed descriptors changed) and
   compare the seven 2%-W curves with the Step-5 curves.
2. If small deviations appear (model differences), re-adjust only the gate WF
   (or the usual calibration knobs) with ND,eff held fixed. This is the
   profile-likelihood test in that setup.
3. Set F: switch the Gaussian to acceptor-like and add its integrated density
   (as implemented in the deck) to ND,eff. I-V and C-V should not change.
4. Thickness: in the same deck, change only the stack (t_ox = 14 nm, or
   t_IWO = 7 nm) and run Step 5 and sets A-E at VD = 0.05 and 1 V. In our
   model the Step-5 Vth (100 nA, VD = 1 V) moves from -0.18 V to -0.25 V for
   t_IWO = 7 nm and to -0.17 V for t_ox = 14 nm (step5_stack_vth.json; ID at
   VG = -1 V is 3.9e-12 A and 4.2e-15 A), so a sweep from about -1 V should be
   close to sufficient (extend it if the off-state is not reached).
   The sets should separate (see "Nonlinear check" above).
5. C-V: quasi-static gate C-V of the original stack at VD = 0 (source and
   drain tied), C = dQ_G/dV_G versus VG, compared as a fraction of C_ox*A, where
   A is the full gate area (L + 2 x 1.5 um overlap, times W).
Each comparison is between descriptor sets in the same deck, so
simulator-specific offsets cancel.
